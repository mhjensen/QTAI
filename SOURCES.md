# QTAI website: sources and scope

Content research was completed on 25 October 2026. Sources support the founder's professional background and teaching topics. They do not establish a commercial customer history for QTAI.

## Company information

The owner described QTAI as a Norwegian limited company concerned with quantum technologies and artificial intelligence. The website uses **QTAI — Quantum Technologies and Artificial Intelligence** as its public name. No exact registered AS name, organisation number, registered office, domain, commercial pricing or customer list was verified. These have not been inferred from the founder's university contact details.

The consulting services, course packages, durations and intended audiences are proposed website offerings. They are not presented as records of previous commercial engagements.

## Proposed course formats

Course topics draw on the teaching and research sources above. The compact company formats, suggested durations, outcomes and audience descriptions are new proposals, to be adapted with the owner and prospective participants. They do not imply university accreditation, a degree qualification, guaranteed hardware access or evidence of quantum advantage.

Links to open course resources are provided for background and further study. The original academic website and some teaching resources state Creative Commons Attribution–NonCommercial 4.0 terms. Each resource retains its own licence. The QTAI site does not copy or relicense an entire course as company property.

## Technical and privacy scope

The website is static HTML, CSS and JavaScript with local assets. It has no analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, persistent browser storage, account system, payment flow, third-party embeds or enquiry backend. The form prepares a draft in the visitor's email application through `mailto:`; it neither sends the email nor stores the enquiry itself. Hosting-server requests and the handling of email after it is sent are separate from that browser-side mechanism and depend on the owner's deployment and email arrangements.

No public deployment or remote Git push is part of this handover. Before launch, the owner should confirm company disclosures, preferred business email, hosting/logging arrangements, enquiry retention and final privacy wording.
