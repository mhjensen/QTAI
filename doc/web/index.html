04 / The expertise behind QTAI

A scientific foundation.
A teaching mindset.

QTAI brings together Morten Hjorth-Jensen’s experience in computational physics, quantum theory and machine learning.

Morten is a Professor of Physics at the University of Oslo. His research spans quantum many-body theory, computational methods, quantum computing and applications of machine learning to the physical sciences.

His teaching portfolio includes extensive open educational material, seminars and software. That combination of research and teaching shapes QTAI’s approach: explain the science clearly, examine the evidence and help people build their own understanding.
