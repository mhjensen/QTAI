QTAI / Website information

Privacy

This website presents QTAI's consulting and course offerings. It uses local fonts, images, styles and scripts. It does not use analytics, advertising trackers, cookies, accounts or persistent browser storage.

Contact enquiries

The enquiry form prepares an email draft in your chosen email application. The website does not send that email, submit your message to an enquiry server or store your form entries. Review the draft before sending it. Your email application or provider may handle a draft according to its own settings and privacy terms.

If you send an enquiry, Morten Hjorth-Jensen receives the information you choose to include, such as your name, email address and message, and uses it to respond to your request and discuss any work you ask about. Avoid including sensitive information that is unnecessary for the enquiry.

Website delivery and external links

The server delivering these pages receives the technical information needed to respond to a page request, including your IP address. Any server logs depend on the hosting configuration; the website does not add visitor profiling or behavioural tracking.

Links to academic profiles, GitHub and teaching resources take you to external websites. Those services apply their own privacy terms when you visit them.

Questions about your information

Contact Morten Hjorth-Jensen at mhjensen@uio.no with questions about this website or information you have provided in an enquiry. You can also ask to correct or delete information from your enquiry.
